Jack Davies // dj swordfish

Jack’s sound is influenced by his work around the world. His sets blend styles and genres, jazzy and soulful, sometimes naughty and never boring.

With a background in events and cultural programming, Jack is adept at responding to a brief, working collaboratively and striking the right tone.

“I love working with clients and venues to find their perfect sound. I’ve been collecting music my whole life. It’s an adventure and it’s a joy to share it with people.”
